## 10 / FAQ — `#faq`

**Section label:** FAQ'S

**Heading:** Ask Now. Relax Later.

**Description:** Straight answers to the questions we hear most, so you can plan your roofing project with confidence.

**Accordion (six questions; the second opens by default, as in the reference):**

1. **How long does a roof replacement take?** Most homes take a few days, depending on roof size, access, materials and weather. You'll get an estimated timeline with your quote, and we'll update you if anything changes.
2. **Do you offer free roof assessments?** Yes. We'll take an honest look at your roof and explain its condition before you decide anything — no pressure, no obligation. *(Confirm this is free before publishing.)*
3. **Can you help after storm damage?** Yes. We can assess the damage, make the roof safe where needed and give you a written report. If you plan to claim on insurance, check your insurer's process and what evidence they need.
4. **Which roofing material is right for my building?** It depends on your roof's structure, the look you want, maintenance expectations and budget. We'll walk you through suitable options and their trade-offs.
5. **Can my roof be repaired instead of replaced?** Often, yes. If the damage is limited and the rest of the roof is sound, a targeted repair is usually the better choice. We'll only recommend replacement when repairs no longer make sense.
6. **What should I do about an urgent leak?** Call us on \[Business phone number\] and describe what's happening. Move valuables away from the leak, catch dripping water if you safely can, and please don't climb onto the roof yourself.
